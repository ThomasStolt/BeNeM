## What the table says

- **Two webhooks for five transitions.** BHNM sent a PROBLEM on open and a RECOVERY on close,
  and nothing for either clear or for the reopen. The reopen produced no notification of any
  kind, which matches what Thomas saw.
- **RENOTIFY is not recorded.** The middleware logs the notification type but not
  `notification_number`, and the push title that would carry "still …" is not logged either. So
  whether the PROBLEM was a first notice cannot be read from the log. With no webhook at the
  reopen, there is no renotification to classify.
- **The closing transition was ALARMS CLEARED→CLOSED, not OPEN→CLOSED.** BHNM cleared at 16:19:35
  and closed five minutes later.
- **The reopen was on screen as OPEN 2 min before Thomas saw red.** The row was served `OPEN` from
  16:06:32.842, and the phone fetched at 16:06:33.122, but Thomas saw red at 16:08:35.
  **[INFERENCE, not logged]** Reopening from the list keeps the counts from the last
  enrichment, which read the alarm UP, and recolour derives the chip from that snapshot. So the
  chip stayed green on an OPEN row until the next detail call read DOWN. Per-incident enrichment
  results are not logged, so this cannot be confirmed from the log.
